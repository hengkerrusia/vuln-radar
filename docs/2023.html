CVE-2023-28131: Expo OAuth proxy open redirect → ATO🧪 lab

2023 — Salt Labs (Salt Security)
Open Redirect

AuthSession proxy (auth.expo.io) stored attacker-influenced returnUrl in an ru cookie, then redirected the OAuth fragment (code+token) to it — the case-sensitive scheme check was bypassed with mixed-case hTTps://.
